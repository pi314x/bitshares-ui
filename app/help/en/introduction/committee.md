# Committee Members

The committee is a set of entities that are approved by the shareholders and
that set policy for the BitShares blockchain. Committee members do not produce
blocks; they steer the parameters the network runs on.

## What the committee decides

* Transaction and trading fees
* Blockchain parameters, such as the block size and the block interval
* Referral and vesting parameters, such as the cashback percentage and the
  vesting periods

## Your vote

Every holder of the BTS token can vote for committee members, and the weight of
a vote equals the number of BTS you own. You can vote yourself or choose a proxy
who votes on your behalf. See [Voting](/help/voting) for details.

# Witnesses

Witnesses are the block producers of the BitShares blockchain. They collect the
transactions made in the network, validate them, and bundle them into new blocks
which they sign. Their role is similar to the role of miners for Bitcoin and
other blockchains, but nobody has to buy mining hardware to become one: a
witness is approved by the shareholders.

## Who may produce blocks

Which witness is allowed to produce a block at which time is decided by a
consensus mechanism called *Delegated Proof of Stake*. Every holder of the BTS
token can cast votes for the witnesses they trust. The witnesses with the most
votes take turns producing blocks, and the list is recomputed every maintenance
interval, so a witness who loses the trust of the shareholders is replaced
quickly.

## Your vote

The weight of your vote equals the number of BTS you own. You can vote for
witnesses yourself or choose a proxy who votes on your behalf. See
[Voting](/help/voting) for details.

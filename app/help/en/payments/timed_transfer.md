# Timed transfer

A timed transfer sends an asset that the recipient can claim only after a date you choose. Nothing is "scheduled": the amount leaves your account when you send it and is locked for the recipient until that moment.

## How it works

 1. Open **Timed transfer** in the menu and choose the recipient, the asset, the amount and the date and time.
 2. Confirm the transaction. The amount is now held in a vesting balance for the recipient. You cannot take it back.
 3. From the chosen time the recipient finds it among their **vesting balances** (account page, Vesting) and can claim it.

## Good to know

 * The date is a point in time on the chain; the recipient can claim in the first block after it.
 * The transfer costs the network fee of creating a vesting balance.
 * Check the recipient's name twice: as with every transfer, the transaction cannot be undone.
